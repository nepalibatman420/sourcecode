Best Foods to Eat in Nepal

Nepal is a country with a rich culinary tradition, and there are many delicious foods that visitors should try during their stay.

Top Must-Try Dishes

  • 1 Dal Bhat: Traditional Nepali meal made up of steamed rice, served with lentil soup (dal), curried vegetables, pickles, and meat.
  • 2 Momo: Steamed or fried dumplings filled with spiced minced meat or vegetables, served with spicy tomato-sesame chutney.
  • 3 Sel Roti: Traditional homemade, ring-shaped sweet rice bread prepared during major festivals.
